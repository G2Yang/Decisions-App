## ✨ Overview

**Decisions** is a mobile social application designed to help users make choices through fast, competitive minigames.  
Players can challenge friends or play against a robot, and the winner gets to **share their decision with a photo** on a social feed.

The app includes:
- Real-time online gameplay using **Firebase**
- Backend authentication and data storage using **Javalin + MySQL**
- A custom camera for sharing decisions
- A complete friend system (add, remove, requests)
- Multilanguage support (7 languages)

### 🎯 Why Decisions?
- **Fun decision-making** instead of arguments  
- **Social interaction** through posts and shared results  
- **Fast minigames** to settle choices  
- **Lightweight and intuitive UI**

## 🚀 Features

- 👥 Add, remove, and manage friends  
- 🔐 Login, register, and password recovery  
- 🎮 Three minigames: Elements, Penalties, Question Quiz  
- 🤖 Play vs friends or vs robot  
- 📸 Custom camera to share decisions  
- 🌍 Multilanguage support (ES, CA, EN, DE, PT, HI, ZH)  
- 🏆 Social feed with photo posts  
- 🔥 Real-time online matches with Firebase  
- 🚫 AFK detection and auto-kick
